What's inside

An end-to-end stack, not a black box.

AiCube ships every layer — silicon drivers, perception pipeline, datalink, ground station, model build, OTA — with the design decisions documented and the code open. Nothing is left as "magic" for you to figure out.

Onboard AI inference

Sophon BM1688 INT8 BModel pipeline runs YOLOv8s detection, segmentation, tracking, and classification at 30+ fps — no GPU, no cloud.

Stereo + thermal VIO

OpenVINS stereo-inertial odometry fuses with the thermal camera for robust pose in low-light and feature-poor environments.

DDL Mesh datalink

Custom binary framing over UDP, AES-256-GCM encryption, ECDH key exchange, with QoS tiers for telemetry vs. video vs. commands.

Flutter GCS

Cross-platform desktop ground station: live video, mission planner, perception overlay, model manager, pre-flight checklist, field-test mode.

Sophon SDK toolchain

Dockerized ONNX→BModel conversion, INT8 calibration, bench harness, and signed manifests. Reproducible builds for every release.

Onboard calibration agent

Camera + IMU calibration runs as a gRPC service on the companion. The GCS drives the wizard and renders the live video — no USB passthrough.

Pre-flight + geofence

Stop-the-line pre-flight checklist across 11 categories, plus polygon geofence editor and upload — enforced on the FC.

OTA, two channels

OS updates via Mender, BModel updates via the datalink with GPG-signed manifests. Hot-swap models without a companion restart.

FC-agnostic

Feature-detects ArduPilot and PX4 at boot; routes geofences, missions, and pre-arm checks to the right MAVLink messages for each.

Live perception HUD

Detection boxes, tracker IDs, classification labels, and geofence boundary all rendered in 60 fps on top of the live video stream.

Post-flight analysis

Pull a rosbag from the companion, replay at 1x, scrub the perception timeline synced to a map of the flight track.

Open source, by design

Every repo Apache-2.0. Every design decision documented. No black-box ML models, no proprietary protocols, no vendor lock-in.

Architecture

Three layers, one cohesive stack.

The companion computer owns the cameras and runs the AI. The dev box builds + signs the BModels. The GCS drives everything from a tablet or laptop and shows the live video + perception overlay.

1

GCS — the operator's window

Flutter desktop app, cross-platform. Reads the live video, drives the mission planner, manages BModels, runs the calibration wizard. The GCS is a thin UI — the heavy work lives on the companion.

2

Companion — the AI at the edge

Sophon BM1688 with 2-core INT8 BModel execution. Owns the USB2 cameras, the VIO node, the perception pipeline, the MAVLink bridge, and the DDL Mesh datalink. Runs two long-lived agents: aicube-calib-agent and aicube-bmodel-agent.

3

Dev box — where the models are born

Linux workstation with Docker. The aicube-bmodel-builder image wraps the Sophon SDK, builds, benchmarks, and signs BModels. Output is pushed to the OTA channel for the companion to install.

4

Flight controller — the muscle

ArduPilot or PX4. AiCube feature-detects the FC at boot and routes geofences, missions, and pre-arm checks to the right MAVLink feature flags.

Workstreams

Eight workstreams, 110 issues, one ship date.

The Phase 1 plan breaks into 8 parallel workstreams tracked in the public GitHub Project. Each card shows the effort estimate and a one-liner.

WS1~10 d

Toolchain

Sophon SDK docker, ONNX→BModel conversion, ML templates, on-device bench harness. Now driven by the GCS.

WS2~8 d

Sensors

Stereo + thermal + radar + IMU drivers, mode-switcher, sensor health. Plus the algorithm side of calibration.

WS3~18 d

Perception

VIO, detection, segmentation, tracking, classification. Hot-swap on model install. Publishes bench results.

WS4~10 d

Datalink

DDL Mesh protocol, Protobuf schemas, encryption, bandwidth governance. RPCs for every feature surface.

WS5~30 d

Ground Control Station

Flutter desktop app: 19 tabs, 6 clusters, the operator's window onto everything. The biggest workstream.

WS6~6 d

Vehicle integration

ArduPilot + PX4 compatibility, geofence upload, mission upload, pre-arm gates.

WS7~5 d

OTA & deployment

Mender for OS, separate signed datalink channel for BModels, A/B partition for safe rollbacks.

WS8~5 d

QA & field testing

Bench regression tests, end-to-end pre-flight tests, two field-test campaigns before ship.

Use cases

Built for the work, not the demo.

AiCube isn't a research toy. It's engineered for the day job — long flights, bad weather, regulatory inspection, and a team that needs the system to come back home.

🛰️

Linear infrastructure inspection

Survey pipelines, power lines, railways. Mission planner + corridor tool + geofence.

🚁

Surveillance & patrol

Detection + tracking + geofence. Hot-swap a fire-detection BModel between shifts.

🌾

Precision agriculture

Multispectral + thermal. Survey waypoints with offline MBTiles. BModel per crop.

🏗️

Construction & mining

Volumetric surveys, progress tracking, safety perimeter enforcement via geofence.

🌊

Search & rescue

Thermal classifier BModel, low-light stereo VIO, long-range DDL Mesh link.

🧪

Research & education

Open source, documented decisions, swappable BModels. Bring your own ONNX.
